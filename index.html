Functional Home Strength Protocols (DB & Med Ball Matched)

Split A: Lower Body Force & Tendons + Steinhoist (Tue AM — Ends 06:30 AM Sharp)

  1. 1. Full-Depth Goblet Squats: 4x8 (35 lb med ball or 35 lb DB held goblet-style; full knee flexion passing parallel)
  2. 2. Single-Leg Dumbbell RDLs: 4x8/leg (35 lb DB; deep posterior chain hip hinge loading glute-hamstring complex)
  3. 3. Loaded Standing Calf Deficit Raises: 4x15 (35 lb DB; full stretch below level into maximum plantarflexion)
  4. 4. Banded Spanish Squats: 3x45s hold (Loop band anchored behind knees; vertical torso for patellar tendon stiffness)
  5. 5. Steinhoisting Extended Holds: 3x Max duration with 5 lb DB extended parallel to floor + 3x20 reps grip tool

Split B: Plyometric Power & Upper Body (Thu AM — Ends 06:30 AM Sharp)

  1. 1. Explosive Countermovement Jumps (CMJs): 3x5 (Performed 100% fresh after dynamic warmup; explosive vertical triple extension)
  2. 2. Strict Dead-Hang Pull-Ups: 4x6–8 (Doorway bar; hollow-body position, full lat engagement)
  3. 3. Single-Arm DB Push Press: 3x8/arm (35 lb DB; athletic dip and drive to powerful overhead lockout)
  4. 4. Deficit Push-Ups: 3x12–15 (Hands elevated on 5 lb DB handles for full pectoral stretch)
  5. 5. Steinhoisting Scapular Wall Drill: 3x60s hold (Posterior deltoid and lower trapezius recruitment against wall)

Split C: Unilateral Core & Trunk Stability (Sat PM or Sun PM)

  1. 1. Walking DB Lunges or Bulgarian Split Squats: 3x10/leg (35 lb DB or med ball; full unilateral drive)
  2. 2. Hanging Knee/Leg Raises: 3x12–15 (Doorway pull-up bar; decompresses spine, complete abdominal compression)
  3. 3. Full RKC Planks: 3x30s max tension (Toes and elbows digging toward center; maximal abdominal bracing)
  4. 4. Banded Pallof Press: 3x12/side (Doorway anchored band; anti-rotational core lock)

*Taper Periodization Rule: In Weeks 13–15, cut volume by 50% (drop from 4 sets to 2 sets across all exercises) but keep the weight high (35 lb implement) to maintain tendon stiffness and motor recruitment into race day.
