⚠️ MASTER REHAB & DECOMPRESSION RULES (RIGHT 3rd/4th TOES):
1. Zero Barefoot Steps: Wear supportive recovery footwear inside the apartment 100% of the time.
2. Metatarsal Pad Positioning: Affix teardrop gel pad immediately proximal (behind) the 2nd–4th metatarsal heads to elevate and spread transverse arch bones.
3. Open-Forefoot Lacing: Thread laces straight across or skip bottom two eyelets to eliminate forefoot transverse compression.
4. Flat-Foot Lifting: Drive all resistance strictly through heels/midfoot. When executing Box Goblet Squats, wrap a loop resistance band around the knees. Drive knees outward against the band to force gluteus medius recruitment. This prevents inward arch collapse (overpronation) and shields the right 3rd/4th intermetatarsal space from lateral compression forces.
5. Active Non-Impact Flushing (The Monday/Friday Coffee Spin): On designated recovery days, utilize 1-2 mile low-resistance outdoor bike rides to local coffee shops. Maintain a high-cadence spinning motion to prevent heavy downstroke forefoot compression. Order clean, unsweetened options (black coffee, cold brew, or plain cortados) to support the metabolic fat-loss track.
