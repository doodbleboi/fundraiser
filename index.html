Smart Budget Strategies & Rules of Thumb

Finding Value On the Grounds

  • Look for the "Every Day Value" Decal: Over 200 items carry the official sticker on vendor menu boards signaling portion-to-price value under $10.
  • Tower Building Portions: The Tower Building Food Court typically offers larger portion-per-dollar values than Midway novelty trailers.
  • Shareable Classics: One $10 Funnel Cake or a $10 basket of fries from Jack's easily feeds 2–3 people as an afternoon snack.
  • Free Tasting Hours: Spend 30 minutes in the GO TEXAN Pavilion sampling Texas-made jams, dips, pecans, and drinks at zero coupon cost.

Beverage & Hydration Rules

  • Bring Reusable Bottles: The Fair allows you to carry in factory-sealed or empty refillable plastic water containers. Tap water refill stations are inside the Tower and Centennial Buildings.
  • Flat $2 Water: Concessions are required to offer standard 16.9 oz bottled water for 2 coupons ($2). Avoid paying $5+ for specialty drinks if thirsty.
  • Refill Souvenir Cups: If buying soda or sweet tea, look for stands offering 5-coupon refills on official souvenir tumblers.
