Split B: Plyometric Power, Upper Force & Deltoid Hypertrophy (Thu AM — Ends 06:30 AM Sharp)

  1. 1. Explosive Countermovement Jumps (CMJs): 3x5 (Performed 100% fresh after dynamic warmup; explosive vertical triple extension)
  2. 2. Strict Dead-Hang Pull-Ups: 4x6–8 (Doorway bar; hollow-body position, full lat engagement for upper V-taper)
  3. 3. Single-Arm DB Push Press: 3x8/arm (35 lb DB; athletic dip and drive to powerful overhead lockout)
  4. 4. DB Lateral Raise Burnout: 4x20–25 reps (Two 5 lb DBs; strict lateral plane abduction, 2-second isometric pause at shoulder height. Targets lateral deltoid head to widen clavicular line and build an athletic shoulder-to-waist ratio)
  5. 5. Deficit Push-Ups: 3x12–15 (Hands elevated on 5 lb DB handles for full pectoral stretch)
  6. 6. Steinhoisting Scapular Wall Drill: 3x60s hold (Posterior deltoid and lower trapezius recruitment against wall)

Split C: Unilateral Core & Trunk Stability (Sat PM or Sun PM)

  1. 1. Walking DB Lunges or Bulgarian Split Squats: 3x10/leg (35 lb DB or med ball; full unilateral drive)
  2. 2. Hanging Knee/Leg Raises: 3x12–15 (Doorway pull-up bar; decompresses spine, complete abdominal compression)
  3. 3. Full RKC Planks: 3x30s max tension (Toes and elbows digging toward center; maximal abdominal bracing)
  4. 4. Banded Pallof Press: 3x12/side (Doorway anchored band; anti-rotational core lock)

*Physiological Rationale: Upper body pulling and lateral deltoid volume builds shoulder width and scapular endurance without adding heavy non-functional mass, stabilizing posture and arm swing mechanics during the late miles of a sub-3:15 marathon while building an athletic V-taper.
