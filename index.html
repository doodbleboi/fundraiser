Dallas Marathon Course Execution

km 1–21 (Miles 1–13): First Half Discipline
Go out controlled at 4:37–4:38/km (7:25–7:28/mi) through downtown and Highland Park. Do not bank time early—save your quads for the back half.
km 22.5–35.4 (Miles 14–22): White Rock Lake & The Hills
Expect rolling climbs around the lake and the steady climb up Dolly Parton / Lawther toward Greenville Ave. Run by effort uphill (allow drift to ~4:43/km (~7:35/mi)) and let the downhills bring you back to 4:33–4:36/km (7:20–7:24/mi).
km 35.4–42.2 (Miles 22–26.2): Final Push to Downtown
Once you crest the final rollers heading back toward downtown, lock into 4:30–4:33/km (7:15–7:20/mi) to clear 3:14:59 with room to spare.
