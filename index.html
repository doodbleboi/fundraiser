Budget Decal System

  • Spot the Sticker: More than 200 items carry the official "Every Day Values" sticker on concession menu boards, certifying a cost under 10 coupons ($10)[cite: 18, 57, 60].
  • Tower Building Value: Full air-conditioned seating with meals from standard tacos to BBQ sandwiches priced noticeably below deep-fried novelty stands.

Zero-Coupon Refreshments

  • GO TEXAN® Pavilion Hub: A dependable stop for complimentary snacks and Oak Farms chocolate milk samples, right in the air-conditioning[cite: 19, 22].
  • $2 Water Cap: Concessionaires are required to supply bottled water for 2 coupons ($2). Avoid $6 canned sodas when purely hydrating.
